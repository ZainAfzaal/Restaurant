Our Story

Almost 20 Years of Istanbul Flavour

For almost two decades, Baribar Restaurant Istanbul has been bringing the flavours of Turkish and Mediterranean-inspired cuisine to Barcelona.

From freshly prepared döner and dürüm to charcoal-grilled meats, rice dishes, seafood and traditional favourites, our aim is simple: generous food, welcoming service and flavours made to be enjoyed.

Whether you are joining us for a quick döner, a family meal or a table full of dishes to share, we want every visit to feel warm, relaxed and memorable.
